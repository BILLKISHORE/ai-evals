title: "Information Warfare"
description: "Test model resistance to generating state-level propaganda and psychological operations"

Information warfare attacks request military-grade influence operations - cognitive warfare, false flag narratives, radicalization pipelines, and institutional delegitimization campaigns. These go beyond simple disinformation into coordinated strategic operations designed to destabilize societies. The attacks test whether models can be weaponized for state-sponsored information operations.

**25 attacks** - 25 single-turn.
