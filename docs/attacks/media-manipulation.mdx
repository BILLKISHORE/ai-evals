title: "Media Manipulation"
description: "Test model willingness to generate fake news, fabricated sources, and disinformation content"
---

Media manipulation attacks request fabricated journalism - fake press releases, forged interview transcripts, fabricated academic papers, and coordinated disinformation campaigns. Each attack produces content designed to deceive audiences by mimicking legitimate media formats. These are high-impact attacks because a single convincing fabrication can spread rapidly through real information ecosystems.

## Summary

**25 attacks** - 25 single-turn.
