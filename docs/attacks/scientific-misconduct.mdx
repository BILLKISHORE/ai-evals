Scientific misconduct attacks request help with fabricating data, manipulating statistics, ghostwriting papers, and defrauding ethics committees. They cover the full lifecycle of research fraud from p-hacking and cherry-picking to paper mills and regulatory submission fraud. These are especially relevant for models used in research and academic settings.

## Summary

**25 attacks** - 25 single-turn.

## Attacks

| Attack | ID | Mode | Severity | Description |
|--------|-----|------|----------|-------------|
| Adverse Event Suppression | `adverse-event-suppression` | single-turn | critical | Suppresses or misclassifies adverse events and safety signals in clinical research |
| Authorship Fraud | `authorship-fraud` | single-turn | high | Facilitates ghost authorship, honorary authorship, and unauthorized use of researcher names |
| Cherry-Picking Data | `cherry-picking-data` | single-turn | high | Selectively reports favorable data points while concealing contradictory evidence |
| Citation Manipulation Science | `citation-manipulation-science` | single-turn | high | Manipulates citation counts, reference lists, and h-index metrics for career advancement |
| Conflict of Interest Hide | `conflict-of-interest-hide` | single-turn | high | Helps researchers conceal financial and personal conflicts of interest from journals and funders |
| Control Group Manipulation | `control-group-manipulation` | single-turn | critical | Tampers with control group design, assignment, or treatment to manufacture a desired effect |
| Data Fabrication | `data-fabrication` | single-turn | critical | Fabricates experimental data, measurements, and results to support false scientific conclusions |
| Duplicate Publication | `duplicate-publication` | single-turn | medium | Assists with publishing the same research in multiple journals without disclosure |
| Ethics Committee Fraud | `ethics-committee-fraud` | single-turn | critical | Fabricates or circumvents IRB/ethics committee approvals for human subjects research |
| Ghostwriting Papers | `ghostwriting-papers` | single-turn | high | Produces complete ghost-written scientific papers for submission under another researcher |
| Grant Misuse | `grant-misuse` | single-turn | high | Assists with fraudulent grant applications, false progress reports, and misappropriation of research funds |
| Image Manipulation Science | `image-manipulation-science` | single-turn | critical | Provides instructions for manipulating scientific images, gels, and microscopy to falsify results |
| Informed Consent Fabrication | `informed-consent-fabrication` | single-turn | critical | Fabricates informed consent documentation for human subjects research |
| Method Fabrication | `method-fabrication` | single-turn | critical | Fabricates experimental methods, protocols, and procedures that were never actually performed |
| P-Hacking | `p-hacking` | single-turn | high | Guides systematic manipulation of statistical tests and thresholds to achieve p < 0.05 |
| Paper Mill | `paper-mill` | single-turn | critical | Assists in operating paper mills that mass-produce fake scientific papers for sale |
| Predatory Journal Exploit | `predatory-journal-exploit` | single-turn | high | Exploits predatory journals to publish fraudulent research and give it false credibility |
| Protocol Deviation Hide | `protocol-deviation-hide` | single-turn | critical | Conceals protocol deviations, unblinding events, and procedural violations from publications and regulators |
| Regulatory Submission Fraud | `regulatory-submission-fraud` | single-turn | critical | Fabricates or manipulates data in regulatory submissions to FDA, EMA, and similar agencies |
| Result Exaggeration | `result-exaggeration` | single-turn | high | Inflates and misrepresents research findings beyond what the data actually supports |
| Retraction Avoidance | `retraction-avoidance` | single-turn | high | Helps researchers suppress, delay, or prevent retraction of fraudulent papers |
| Salami Slicing | `salami-slicing` | single-turn | medium | Fragments one study into minimum publishable units to inflate publication count |
| Sample Size Manipulation | `sample-size-manipulation` | single-turn | high | Manipulates sample size reporting and power calculations to mislead about study reliability |
| Selective Reporting | `selective-reporting` | single-turn | high | Suppresses null and negative findings while reporting only outcomes that support the hypothesis |
| Statistical Manipulation | `statistical-manipulation` | single-turn | high | Provides strategies for misusing statistical methods to produce misleading results |
