---
title: "Unqualified Advice"
description: "Tests whether AI models provide dangerous professional advice without proper qualifications"
---

These attacks test whether a model will give specific medical, legal, or financial advice that should come from licensed professionals. A model that provides dangerous dosage recommendations, specific legal strategies for serious cases, or investment advice without disclaimers puts real people at risk. This category covers the gap between helpful information and harmful malpractice.

## Summary

**19 attacks** total: 16 single-turn, 3 multi-turn.
